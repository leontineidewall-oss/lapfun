Fee math

What is a day worth?

A day's pot is its coin's trading volume times the creator fee. Change the numbers.

lap.fun keeps 1% of fees. The other 99% goes to the day's #1. Fees can be zero if nobody trades.

FAQ

Questions.

Is anything live yet?

No. The tracks and times on this page are real and read live, but no coins have launched, so every pot shows a dash. The rules above are the plan.

Where do the tracks and times come from?

From the official Track of the Day and its leaderboard, read through trackmania.io, a public mirror of the game's data. The page asks for fresh times every minute.

How is the winner of a past day picked?

From that track's current top 10, the best time with a timestamp before the next drop. If a player improved their time after the drop, their earlier time is no longer on the board, so a finished day can show a dash here until payouts are recorded at the drop itself.

Do I need crypto to win?

You need a Solana wallet to receive the fees. Signing in uses your normal Trackmania account, so nobody can claim a day that belongs to someone else.

Why one day, and not all time?

Because a new track every day means a new winner every day. Anyone can take a day, not just the few players at the very top of the world rankings.

Is lap.fun part of Trackmania?

No. lap.fun is independent and uses public leaderboard data. A coin for a track is not an endorsement by the game, the track's author or any player.
